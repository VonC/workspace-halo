0.0.24-SNAPSHOT -- Observed, Not Ordered

The host trusts IsIconic readings over WinEvents that arrive late

- Three Monitors to One, One Restore per Minimize
  -- Unplugging a dock no longer sends VS Code windows back to the front
- Nine Files and Twelve Advisories
  -- A VSIX without review files and a lockfile past its CVEs

This release fixes the minimize loop seen after a monitor unplug. The
native host used to decide from WinEvent order, so minimize events that
Windows delivered seconds late, or reordered, after a display change
restarted the thumbnail-halo replay and restored windows nobody asked for.
The host now reads IsIconic on every 25 ms tick, on each minimize event
and around its own ShowWindow calls, and intercepts only a shown-to-iconic
edge it did not cause, observed within 500 ms. Minimizes of unknown age,
an own call that stays unsettled for 1 s, and more than two interceptions
within 2 s per window go through without the halo, and every decision is
one line in native-host.log.

The release also tidies what ships and how it is built. The VSIX now holds
only the manifest, the documents, the icon, the bundled extension and the
native host, instead of 308 files that included the local review folder.
The fast-uri, js-yaml and undici lockfile entries used by the packaging
tool move past twelve Dependabot advisories, and senv.bat repairs a stale
npm-lock filter path on its own.

### Key changes

- **Observed-edge minimize interception**: the pure model in
  minimize_windows.go and the hook controller in minimize_hook_windows.go
  replace the event-order state machine, with a 500 ms lateness bound, a
  1 s settle timeout, a session latch and a per-window cap of two
  interceptions in 2 s, rearmed after 5 s of quiet. Two manual
  three-to-one unplugs on the committed build show at most one restore per
  minimize and no window coming back on its own.

- **A VSIX with only the extension runtime**: .vscodeignore now excludes
  .reviews/, the docs/vX.Y.Z/ effort folders, .review-validation,
  install.bat and version.bat, so the package drops from 308 files to 9
  and no review artifact or step note leaves the machine that built it.

- **Packaging toolchain past twelve advisories**: lockfile-only updates of
  fast-uri to 3.1.8 (CVE-2026-18446, CVE-2026-75931, CVE-2026-75899,
  CVE-2026-75975, CVE-2026-76172), js-yaml to 4.3.2 (CVE-2026-84375 and
  GHSA-5p4m-2wfm-xmqj) and undici to 7.30.0 (CVE-2026-13697,
  CVE-2026-16728, CVE-2026-16729, CVE-2026-14643, CVE-2026-15157), all
  pulled in by @vscode/vsce and never packaged into the extension.
